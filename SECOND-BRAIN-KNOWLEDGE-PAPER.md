# SECOND-BRAIN-KNOWLEDGE-PAPER.md — Mental Wellness & Mindfulness Self-Reflection Advisor

A curated knowledge base of research papers, books, and authoritative sources underpinning this skill's methodology. These should be consulted (and their principles extracted into skill reference files) when building the actual skill.

> **Note on sourcing:** This list was compiled from general subject-matter knowledge to give the skill a strong research foundation to build from. Before relying on any specific citation (e.g., in an academic, legal, or professional deliverable), verify the exact title, year, and venue independently, as this list has not been individually re-verified against live databases at compilation time.

## Research Papers & Sources (20)

1. A. Beck (1979). Cognitive Therapy and the Emotional Disorders. International Universities Press — Foundational CBT framework.
2. S. Hayes, K. Strosahl, K. Wilson (1999). Acceptance and Commitment Therapy: An Experiential Approach to Behavior Change. Guilford Press — Foundational ACT framework.
3. J. Kabat-Zinn (1990). Full Catastrophe Living: Using the Wisdom of Your Body and Mind to Face Stress, Pain, and Illness. Delacorte — Foundational MBSR framework.
4. M. Seligman (2011). Flourish: A Visionary New Understanding of Happiness and Well-Being. Free Press — Origin of the PERMA wellbeing model.
5. D. Kabat-Zinn, J. Kabat-Zinn (2013). Mindfulness Meditation for Everyday Life. Hachette — Applied mindfulness psychoeducation.
6. S. Hofmann, A. Sawyer, A. Witt, D. Oh (2010). The Effect of Mindfulness-Based Therapy on Anxiety and Depression: A Meta-Analytic Review. Journal of Consulting and Clinical Psychology — Evidence base for mindfulness-based approaches.
7. World Health Organization (2004). Promoting Mental Health: Concepts, Emerging Evidence, Practice. WHO — Framework distinguishing mental-health promotion from clinical diagnosis/treatment.
8. C. Peterson, M. Seligman (2004). Character Strengths and Virtues: A Handbook and Classification. Oxford University Press — Strengths-based psychoeducation framework.
9. R. Ryan, E. Deci (2000). Self-Determination Theory and the Facilitation of Intrinsic Motivation, Social Development, and Well-Being. American Psychologist — Foundational wellbeing/motivation theory.
10. J. Pennebaker (1997). Writing About Emotional Experiences as a Therapeutic Process. Psychological Science — Evidence base for reflective-journaling technique.
11. Z. Segal, M. Williams, J. Teasdale (2002). Mindfulness-Based Cognitive Therapy for Depression. Guilford Press — MBCT framework informing safe psychoeducational design (not for self-diagnosis use).
12. S. Hayes (2004). Acceptance and Commitment Therapy, Relational Frame Theory, and the Third Wave of Behavioral and Cognitive Therapies. Behavior Therapy — Theoretical basis of ACT's 'third wave' approach.
13. K. Neff (2003). Self-Compassion: An Alternative Conceptualization of a Healthy Attitude Toward Oneself. Self and Identity — Self-compassion framework for wellbeing psychoeducation.
14. World Health Organization (2019). Mental Health Action Plan 2013-2020 (extended). WHO — Global mental-health promotion policy framework, referenced for safe-scope design.
15. B. Fredrickson (2001). The Role of Positive Emotions in Positive Psychology: The Broaden-and-Build Theory of Positive Emotions. American Psychologist — Positive-psychology theoretical foundation.
16. WHO/IASP guidance summarized in International Association for Suicide Prevention (2021). Media and Communication Guidelines. IASP — Basis for crisis-resource-surfacing safety design.
17. S. Hofmann, G. Asmundson (2008). Acceptance and Mindfulness-Based Therapy: New Wave or Old Hat? Clinical Psychology Review — Comparative review situating ACT/mindfulness among psychoeducational approaches.
18. E. Diener (2000). Subjective Well-Being: The Science of Happiness and a Proposal for a National Index. American Psychologist — Foundational subjective-wellbeing measurement research.
19. J. Teasdale et al. (2000). Prevention of Relapse/Recurrence in Major Depression by Mindfulness-Based Cognitive Therapy. Journal of Consulting and Clinical Psychology — Evidence base cited to justify professional-referral emphasis for clinical-level symptoms.
20. National Institute for Health and Care Excellence (NICE) (2011). Common Mental Health Problems: Identification and Pathways to Care. NICE Guideline — Standard for appropriate professional-referral pathways referenced in skill safety design.
