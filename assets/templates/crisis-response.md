# Crisis Response Templates — Immediate Safety Resources

**Purpose:** Provide standardized, compassionate crisis response templates that immediately surface resources. These are safety-critical templates requiring precise, consistent use.

> **Use these templates when:** Any crisis keyword is detected, user expresses suicidal/homicidal ideation, or any other safety concern is present.

## Tier 1: Immediate Crisis Response (Severe)

**When to use:** Severe crisis keywords detected (suicide, self-harm plan, etc.)

```
**I hear that you're going through a very difficult time, and I want you to know that your life matters.**

You are not alone, and there are people who want to help you through this.

---

**IMMEDIATE CRISIS RESOURCES**

**If you are in immediate danger, please call emergency services (911 in the US) or go to your nearest emergency room.**

**United States:**
- **988 Suicide & Crisis Lifeline:** Dial or text 988 (available 24/7, free, confidential)
- **Crisis Text Line:** Text HOME to 741741 (available 24/7)
- **Veterans Crisis Line:** Dial 988 then press 1, or text 838255
- **National Suicide Prevention Lifeline:** 1-800-273-TALK (8255)

**Canada:**
- **Talk Suicide Canada:** 1-833-456-4566
- **Crisis Services Canada:** 1-833-456-4566

**United Kingdom:**
- **Samaritans:** 116 123
- **Shout Crisis Text Line:** Text SHOUT to 85258
- **Childline (for under 19s):** 0800 1111

**Australia:**
- **Lifeline:** 13 11 14
- **Suicide Call Back Service:** 1300 659 467
- **Kids Helpline (for ages 5-25):** 1800 55 1800

**International Resources:**
- **Find A Helpline:** https://findahelpline.com/ (search by country, available 24/7)
- **Befrienders Worldwide:** https://www.befrienders.org/ (crisis support worldwide)

---

**You are not alone.** Help is available 24/7, and people care about you. Please reach out to one of these resources or contact a trusted adult, friend, or family member.

**This service is not equipped to handle crisis situations.** For immediate support, please use the resources listed above or go to your nearest emergency room.

**What to expect when you call or text:**
- Trained crisis counselors who will listen without judgment
- Support to help you stay safe right now
- Connection to local resources for ongoing support
- Confidentiality (except in life-threatening situations)

**If reaching out feels hard:**
- Start with "I'm having a hard time and need help"
- You don't have to explain everything at once
- The person on the other end wants to help
- Taking this step is a sign of strength, not weakness

---

**Disclaimer:** This response provides crisis resources for informational purposes. It is not a substitute for emergency services or professional mental health care. If you or someone you know is in danger, please contact emergency services immediately.
```

## Tier 2: Elevated Distress Response (Moderate)

**When to use:** Moderate crisis keywords (hopeless, burden, etc.) or elevated distress detected

```
**I can hear that you're going through a really difficult time right now, and I want you to know there are people who can help.**

---

**SUPPORT RESOURCES**

**If you need to talk with someone now:**
- **988 Suicide & Crisis Lifeline:** Dial or text 988 (US, 24/7, free, confidential)
- **Crisis Text Line:** Text HOME to 741741 (24/7)
- **International:** https://findahelpline.com/

**For professional support:**
- **Psychology Today:** https://www.psychologytoday.com/ (find therapists by location, insurance, specialty)
- **Your Insurance:** Call the number on your insurance card for in-network providers
- **Primary Care Doctor:** Can provide referrals and assessments
- **Employee Assistance Program (EAP):** If your workplace offers one

**For crisis situations:**
- If you're in immediate danger, call 911 (US) or 999 (UK) or go to your nearest emergency room
- These feelings are real, treatable, and you don't have to face them alone

---

**What you're experiencing is valid, and support is available.** Many people face similar challenges and find help through professional support, crisis lines, or trusted people in their lives.

**This service is not equipped to handle crisis situations.** If you're experiencing thoughts of self-harm or suicide, please reach out to one of the crisis resources listed above.

---

**Disclaimer:** This response provides general information and support resources. It is not a substitute for professional mental health care. If you're in crisis or having thoughts of self-harm, please contact a crisis resource or emergency services immediately.
```

## Tier 3: Supportive Response (Mild)

**When to use:** Mild distress keywords (overwhelmed, stressed) or general support needed

```
**I hear that things are really hard right now, and I want you to know that support is available.**

---

**RESOURCES FOR SUPPORT**

**For someone to talk to:**
- **988 Suicide & Crisis Lifeline:** Dial or text 988 (US, 24/7, free)
- **Crisis Text Line:** Text HOME to 741741 (24/7)
- **International:** https://findahelpline.com/

**For ongoing support:**
- **Therapist or Counselor:** Can provide consistent support and tools
- **Support Groups:** Connect with others facing similar challenges
- **Trusted Friends/Family:** Sometimes just talking helps

**For professional guidance:**
- **Psychology Today:** https://www.psychologytoday.com/
- **Your Doctor:** Can assess and refer to appropriate care
- **Mental Health Screenings:** Available through many organizations

---

**What you're feeling right now makes sense given what you're going through.** Many people find that talking with someone — a friend, family member, or professional — makes a real difference.

**You don't have to figure this out alone.** Support is available, and reaching out is a sign of strength.

---

**Disclaimer:** This response provides general information and resources. It is not professional advice or mental health care. For ongoing support, consider connecting with a qualified mental health professional.
```

## Situation-Specific Crisis Responses

### For Suicidal Thoughts

```
**Thank you for sharing something so difficult. Your honesty takes courage, and I want you to know that help is available.**

---

**IMMEDIATE SUPPORT**

**If you're in danger right now, please call 911 (US) or 999 (UK) or go to your nearest emergency room.**

**Talk with someone now:**
- **988 Suicide & Crisis Lifeline:** Dial or text 988 (US, 24/7)
- **Crisis Text Line:** Text HOME to 741741
- **International:** https://findahelpline.com/

**What to expect when you call:**
- A trained crisis counselor will listen without judgment
- They'll help you stay safe right now
- They can connect you to local ongoing support
- Everything is confidential except in life-threatening situations

---

**Please reach out to one of these resources or contact a trusted person in your life.** You deserve support, and people want to help.

**If you're not sure about calling, that's OK.** Sometimes taking that first step is the hardest part. The people on the other end want to listen and help.

**This service cannot provide crisis support.** Please use the resources above for immediate help.

---

**Disclaimer:** This response provides crisis resources. It is not emergency care or mental health treatment.
```

### For Self-Harm Thoughts

```
**Thank you for sharing this. I want you to know that support is available and you don't have to handle this alone.**

---

**GETTING SUPPORT NOW**

**If you've injured yourself and need medical help, please call 911 (US) or go to your nearest emergency room.**

**Talk with someone:**
- **988 Suicide & Crisis Lifeline:** Dial or text 988 (US, 24/7)
- **Crisis Text Line:** Text HOME to 741741
- **Self-Harm Hotline:** 1-800-DONT-CUT (1-800-366-8288) (US)
- **International:** https://findahelpline.com/

**For professional support:**
- A therapist can help you understand what's underneath the self-harm
- They can teach you other ways to cope with difficult feelings
- Recovery is possible, and people want to help

---

**What you're experiencing is valid, and there are other ways to cope.** Self-harm often serves a purpose — it might help you feel something, or nothing at all, or release tension. Understanding what purpose it serves is the first step toward finding other ways to meet that need.

**You deserve support, and people want to help you through this.**

**This service cannot provide crisis support.** Please use the resources above for help.

---

**Disclaimer:** This response provides resources. It is not medical care or mental health treatment.
```

### For Harming Others Thoughts

```
**Thank you for sharing this. It takes courage to reach out, and I want to connect you with people who can help.**

---

**GETTING SUPPORT NOW**

**If you're in danger of hurting someone or have a plan, please call 911 (US) or 999 (UK) immediately.**

**Talk with someone:**
- **988 Suicide & Crisis Lifeline:** Dial or text 988 (US, 24/7)
- **Crisis Text Line:** Text HOME to 741741
- **Emergency Services:** 911 (US), 999 (UK), 000 (Australia)

**For professional support:**
- A mental health professional can help you understand these thoughts
- They can teach you ways to manage difficult feelings
- Treatment is available and effective

---

**Having these thoughts doesn't mean you're a bad person.** It means you're experiencing something difficult, and people want to help you work through it.

**Please reach out to one of the resources above or contact emergency services if needed.**

**This service cannot provide crisis support.** Please use emergency or crisis resources for immediate help.

---

**Disclaimer:** This response provides resources. It is not legal or medical advice or mental health treatment.
```

## Post-Crisis Follow-Up

### After Crisis Contact

```
**I'm glad you reached out for support. That took courage, and I hope you're connected with people who can help.**

---

**NEXT STEPS**

**If you spoke with a crisis line:**
- They may have provided resources or follow-up contacts
- Save those numbers somewhere safe
- Consider reaching out to them again if needed

**For ongoing support:**
- **Therapist:** Regular sessions can provide consistent support
- **Support Groups:** Connect with others who understand
- **Trusted People:** Identify friends/family you can call

**Safety Planning:**
- Identify warning signs that you might need help
- List people you can contact
- Remove access to means if needed
- Create a plan for crisis moments

**Self-Care:**
- Rest and recover after crisis moments
- Be gentle with yourself
- Take things one step at a time
- Celebrate reaching out — that's strength

---

**You're not alone in this.** Support is available, and people care about you.

---

**Disclaimer:** This response provides general information. It is not professional mental health care.
```

## Quality Checklist

Before providing crisis response, verify:

- [ ] Crisis tier appropriate to detected keywords
- [ ] All resources accurate and current
- [ ] Multiple contact options provided
- [ ] International resources included if location unknown
- [ ] Compassionate, non-judgmental tone
- [ ] Clear instructions for getting help
- [ ] Appropriate disclaimer included
- [ ] No diagnostic language used
- [ ] Hope and connection emphasized
- [ ] Limitations of service clearly stated

## Crisis Response Protocol

### Detection to Response Flow

```
1. CRISIS DETECTION
   - Scan input for crisis keywords
   - Assess severity tier
   - Identify specific type (suicide, self-harm, etc.)

2. IMMEDIATE RESPONSE
   - Provide crisis response template
   - Include appropriate resources
   - Maintain compassionate tone
   - Set clear service boundaries

3. FOLLOW-UP ENCOURAGEMENT
   - Encourage professional connection
   - Suggest trusted person contact
   - Provide ongoing resource options
   - Normalize help-seeking

4. DOCUMENTATION
   - Log crisis detection
   - Record response provided
   - Note any follow-up needs
   - Audit safety system
```
