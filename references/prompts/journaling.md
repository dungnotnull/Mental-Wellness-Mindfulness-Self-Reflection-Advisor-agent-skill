# Journaling Prompts — Reflective Self-Exploration

**Purpose:** Provide structured journaling prompts grounded in CBT/ACT frameworks for emotional processing, self-reflection, and values exploration.

> **Use these prompts for:** Daily journaling, emotional processing, values clarification, cognitive restructuring, and personal growth.

## Prompt Organization

- **General Reflection:** Open-ended prompts for any situation
- **CBT-Focused:** Cognitive restructuring and thought examination
- **ACT-Focused:** Values, defusion, and psychological flexibility
- **Emotion-Specific:** Tailored for particular emotional experiences
- **Situation-Specific:** For common life situations

## General Reflection Prompts

### Daily Check-In

```
1. What's present in your awareness right now?
   - Thoughts?
   - Emotions?
   - Body sensations?

2. What mattered most to you today?

3. What was challenging, and how did you respond?

4. What are you grateful for, even in a small way?

5. What's one thing you want to remember from today?
```

### Situation Exploration

```
1. SITUATION: What happened?
   - Describe objectively, like a camera recording

2. AUTOMATIC THOUGHTS: What went through your mind?
   - Capture exact thoughts
   - Rate belief in each (0-100%)

3. EMOTIONS: What did you feel?
   - Name each emotion
   - Rate intensity (0-100)

4. BODY: What sensations did you notice?
   - Tightness, warmth, movement, etc.

5. VALUES: What mattered to you in this situation?
   - What kind of person did you want to be?

6. ALTERNATIVE PERSPECTIVES: What else might be true?
   - What evidence supports your thoughts?
   - What evidence contradicts them?
   - What would you tell a friend?

7. ACTION: What next step aligns with your values?
   - Even a small, imperfect action
```

### Weekly Reflection

```
1. What values did you live this week?

2. What values did you move away from?

3. What patterns do you notice in your thoughts/feelings?

4. What helped you this week?

5. What made things harder?

6. What do you want to carry forward?

7. What do you want to leave behind?

8. What's one values-aligned action for next week?
```

## CBT-Focused Prompts

### Thought Record (Full)

```
1. SITUATION
   What happened? (Objective description)

2. AUTOMATIC THOUGHT
   What thought went through your mind?
   How much do you believe it? (0-100%)

3. EMOTION
   What emotion(s) did you feel?
   How intense? (0-100 each)

4. COGNITIVE DISTORTION CHECK
   Are any distortions present?
   - All-or-nothing thinking?
   - Overgeneralization?
   - Mental filter?
   - Disqualifying the positive?
   - Jumping to conclusions?
   - Magnification/catastrophizing?
   - Emotional reasoning?
   - Should statements?
   - Labeling?
   - Personalization/blame?

5. EVIDENCE
   Evidence FOR the thought:
   Evidence AGAINST the thought:

6. ALTERNATIVE THOUGHT
   What's a more balanced thought?
   How much do you believe it? (0-100%)

7. OUTCOME
   How do you feel now?
   Emotion intensity now: (0-100)
   What action aligns with your values?
```

### Behavioral Activation

```
1. List activities you used to enjoy or might enjoy:
   [Make a list of 10+ activities]

2. For each activity, rate:
   - PLEASURE: How enjoyable might it be? (0-10)
   - MASTERY: How capable are you? (0-10)
   - EASE: How easy is it to do? (0-10)

3. Choose 3 activities to schedule this week:
   Activity: ___________ When: ___________
   Activity: ___________ When: ___________
   Activity: ___________ When: ___________

4. After doing each, re-rate:
   - Actual pleasure: (0-10)
   - Actual mastery: (0-10)
   - Would you do it again? (Yes/No)

5. What patterns do you notice?
```

### Anxiety-Focused CBT

```
1. What anxiety-provoking situation are you facing?

2. What are you afraid will happen?
   (Worst-case scenario)

3. What's the evidence this will happen?

4. What's the evidence it won't happen or won't be as bad?

5. What's the MOST LIKELY outcome?
   (Not best or worst, but realistic)

6. If the worst did happen, how could you cope?
   - What resources do you have?
   - What have you survived before?
   - Who could help?

7. What's one small step you could take?

8. What would you tell a friend with this fear?
```

### Depression-Focused CBT

```
1. What thought is contributing to low mood?

2. What evidence do you have that it's completely true?

3. What evidence do you have that it's NOT 100% true?

4. What would you say to a friend with this thought?

5. What's one small action that might help, even a little?

6. What's one thing that was OK today, even briefly?

7. What evidence do you have that things will always feel this way?

8. What's one thing you're looking forward to, however small?
```

## ACT-Focused Prompts

### Values Clarification (Full)

```
DOMAIN: [Family/Friendships/Work/Health/Growth/Community/Leisure/Spirituality]

1. WHAT MATTERS
   What do you value in this domain?
   List 3-5 core values:

2. VISION
   What kind of person do you want to be in this area?
   Free-write your ideal:

3. CURRENT REALITY
   How are you currently living these values?
   Rate your current alignment: (1-10)

4. GAP
   What's the difference between your vision and reality?

5. BARRIERS
   What gets in the way?
   - Thoughts?
   - Feelings?
   - Situations?

6. DEFUSION
   Can you notice these barriers without struggling?
   What if you made space for them while moving forward?

7. COMMITTED ACTION
   What's one specific, achievable step this week?
   Even a small, imperfect action:
```

### Defusion Practice

```
1. DIFFICULT THOUGHT
   What troubling thought is present?
   Write it down:

2. LABELING
   Practice: "I'm having the thought that..."
   Complete: "I'm having the thought that..."

3. SILLY VOICE
   Say this thought in a silly voice (cartoon character, opera singer)
   Notice how the feeling changes:

4. LEAVES ON STREAM
   Imagine this thought written on a leaf
   Watch it float down the stream
   Notice new leaves arriving

5. OBSERVER
   Ask: "Who is noticing this thought?"
   Rest in the awareness of being the observer

6. NEXT ACTION
   What matters to you right now?
   What small action aligns with your values?
```

### Acceptance Practice

```
1. DIFFICULT EXPERIENCE
   What thought, feeling, or sensation is present?
   Describe it:

2. STRUGGLE CHECK
   Are you struggling with this experience?
   How? (Avoiding, fighting, suppressing?)

3. MAKING SPACE
   Can you instead make space for it?
   Breathe around it
   Allow it to be present

4. DROPPING THE ROPE
   Imagine you're in a tug-of-war
   What if you dropped the rope?
   Notice what happens

5. VALUES-BASED ACTION
   What matters to you in this situation?
   What action aligns with your values?
   Even with this difficult experience present?

6. SMALL STEP
   What's one tiny step toward your values?
   Something you can do right now
```

### Present-Moment Awareness

```
1. GROUNDING (5-4-3-2-1)
   - 5 things you can SEE
   - 4 things you can TOUCH
   - 3 things you can HEAR
   - 2 things you can SMELL
   - 1 thing you can TASTE

2. NOTICING
   What thoughts are present?
   What emotions are present?
   What body sensations are present?

3. OBSERVING
   Notice that there is a YOU noticing all of this
   Rest in that awareness for a moment

4. RETURNING
   What's happening right now?
   What matters in this moment?
```

### Committed Action Planning

```
1. VALUE
   What value matters in this situation?

2. GOAL
   What action would align with this value?

3. BARRIERS
   What might get in the way?
   Thoughts? Feelings? Situations?

4. DEFUSION/ACCEPTANCE
   How can you make space for barriers while taking action?

5. SMART ACTION
   Specific: What exactly will you do?
   Measurable: How will you know it's done?
   Achievable: Is it realistic?
   Relevant: Does it connect to your values?
   Time-bound: When will you do it?

6. EXPERIMENT
   Take the action
   Notice what happens
   What did you learn?
```

### Anxiety

```
1. What are you anxious about?

2. What are you afraid will happen?

3. What's the evidence this will happen?

4. What's the most likely outcome?

5. What coping skills do you have?

6. What can you control in this situation?

7. What's one small step you could take?

8. What would you tell a friend with this anxiety?
```

### Sadness/Depression

```
1. What's contributing to feeling sad?

2. What thoughts are present?

3. What happened before the sadness started?

4. What might this sadness be telling you matters?

5. What's one thing that was OK, even briefly?

6. What small action might help, even a little?

7. What would you tell a friend feeling this way?

8. What support do you have?
```

### Anger

```
1. What happened that triggered anger?

2. What values were violated?

3. What's underneath the anger?
   - Hurt? Fear? Sadness? Injustice?

4. What's the anger trying to protect or achieve?

5. What action would align with your values?

6. How can you express this effectively?

7. What boundaries need to be set?

8. What would help resolve this situation?
```

### Guilt/Shame

```
1. What happened that led to these feelings?

2. Was there actual harm done?
   - If yes: How can you repair it?
   - If no: Can you forgive yourself?

3. What values does this connect to?
   - What kind of person do you want to be?

4. What did you learn?

5. What repair is needed, if any?

6. What self-compassion is needed?

7. What action aligns with your values now?

8. What would you tell a friend in this situation?
```

### Fear

```
1. What are you afraid of?

2. What's the worst that could happen?

3. How likely is that? (0-100%)

4. If it happened, how would you cope?

5. What resources do you have?

6. What's the most likely outcome?

7. What's one small step toward facing this?

8. What would make this situation easier?
```

```
1. What happened? (Objective description)

2. What mattered to you in this situation?

3. What values were involved?

5. What thoughts are present about yourself?
   About the other person?

6. What emotions are present?

7. What would your best self do in this situation?

8. What action aligns with your values?

9. How can you communicate effectively?

10. What repair is needed, if any?
```

### Decision-Making

```
1. What decision are you facing?

2. What options do you have?

3. What values are at stake?

4. What does each option align with?

5. What are the likely outcomes of each?

6. What risks exist?

7. What would your best self choose?

8. What does your intuition say?

9. What information do you still need?

10. What small step moves you forward?

```

```
6. What's within your control?

7. What support do you have?
```

### Setback/Failure


### Achievement/Success

## Using These Prompts Effectively

### Guidelines

1. **Be Honest:** Write without filtering or judgment
2. **Be Specific:** Include concrete details and examples
3. **Be Consistent:** Regular practice builds awareness
4. **Be Kind:** Treat yourself with compassion
5. **Be Patient:** Change takes time

### When to Journal

- **Morning:** Set intentions, check in with yourself
- **Evening:** Process the day, identify patterns
- **After Events:** Process difficult or meaningful experiences
- **Weekly:** Review patterns, set intentions
- **As Needed:** Anytime you need clarity

### Safety Note

If journaling brings up:
- Crisis thoughts (self-harm, suicide)
- Overwhelming distress
- Trauma memories

**Stop journaling and reach out for professional support.**

These prompts are educational tools, not treatment. For ongoing support, consider working with a licensed mental health professional.
