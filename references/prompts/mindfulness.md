# Mindfulness Scripts — MBSR-Informed Exercises

**Purpose:** Provide guided mindfulness, breathing, and stress-reduction exercises grounded in MBSR principles. These are educational practices, not clinical treatments.

> **Use these scripts for:** Daily mindfulness practice, stress reduction, present-moment awareness, and emotion regulation.

## Script Organization

- **Breathing Exercises:** Quick practices for regulation
- **Body-Based Practices:** Grounding and awareness
- **Meditation Scripts:** Formal mindfulness practices
- **Stress-Reduction:** Specific techniques for difficult moments
- **Daily Life:** Informal mindfulness practices

## Breathing Exercises

### Basic Breath Awareness (5 minutes)

**Purpose:** Anchor in the present moment through breath

```
Find a comfortable position, sitting or lying down.
Let your body settle and relax.

Bring your attention to your breath.
Notice where you feel it most clearly—
nostrils, chest, or belly.

No need to change your breath,
just notice it as it naturally flows.

In... and out.
Rising... and falling.

Notice the temperature of the air.
Cool as you inhale,
warm as you exhale.

Notice the rhythm—
fast or slow, deep or shallow.
Just observing.

When your mind wanders—
to thoughts, plans, memories—
simply notice that it wandered.
Gently, without judgment,
return your attention to the breath.

In... and out.
Here... and now.

Continue for the full duration,
noticing each wandering,
and each return to presence.

When you're ready,
slowly open your eyes
and notice how you feel.
```

### 4-4-4-4 Box Breathing (5 minutes)

**Purpose:** Create balance and focus

```
Sit comfortably with your back straight.
Rest your hands in your lap.
Close your eyes or soften your gaze.

Inhale slowly through your nose
1... 2... 3... 4...

Hold gently at the top
1... 2... 3... 4...

Exhale slowly through your nose
1... 2... 3... 4...

Hold gently at the bottom
1... 2... 3... 4...

Inhale 1... 2... 3... 4...
Hold 1... 2... 3... 4...
Exhale 1... 2... 3... 4...
Hold 1... 2... 3... 4...

Continue this rhythm,
feeling the balance of each phase.

Notice your mind settling
with each cycle.

Continue for the full practice,
then release the counting
and breathe naturally.

Notice the effects—
calmer? Clearer? More present?

When you're ready,
open your eyes
and carry this sense of balance
into your day.
```

### Diaphragmatic Breathing (5 minutes)

**Purpose:** Activate relaxation response

```
Place one hand on your chest,
one hand on your belly.

Sit comfortably or lie down.

Take a slow breath in through your nose,
feeling your belly rise.
Your chest should move minimally.

Exhale slowly through your mouth,
feeling your belly fall.
Make the exhale longer than the inhale.

Inhale... belly rises 2... 3... 4...
Exhale... belly falls 2... 3... 4... 5... 6

Inhale... 2... 3... 4...
Exhale... 2... 3... 4... 5... 6

Continue breathing into your belly,
feeling the expansion and contraction.

Notice your hands moving—
belly hand rising and falling,
chest hand relatively still.

This deep breathing signals safety
to your nervous system,
activating relaxation.

Continue for the full duration,
then return to normal breathing.

Notice the effects—
more relaxed? Calmer? Present?
```

### Coherent Breathing (5-5 or 6-6 rhythm) (10 minutes)

**Purpose:** Optimize heart rate variability

```
Sit comfortably with spine straight.

Inhale slowly for 5 counts
1... 2... 3... 4... 5...

Exhale slowly for 5 counts
1... 2... 3... 4... 5...

Continue this smooth rhythm,
no pauses, no holding.

Just a gentle wave of breath—
in... out...
in... out...

Find a pace that feels effortless,
smooth, and continuous.

Let your breath find its own rhythm
within the 5-5 count.

Notice your body relaxing
with each cycle.

Notice your mind settling
into the rhythm.

Continue for the full duration,
then return to normal breathing.

Notice any sense of flow,
balance, or ease
you can carry forward.
```

## Body-Based Practices

### Body Scan (15 minutes)

**Purpose:** Systematic body awareness and relaxation

```
Lie down comfortably on your back.
Allow your legs to uncross,
arms at your sides,
eyes closed or softly focused.

Take a few deep breaths,
feeling your body supported by the floor.

Bring attention to the top of your head.
Notice any sensations—
warmth, coolness, tingling, contact.

No need to change anything,
just notice what's here.

Slowly move attention down...
Your forehead—any tension?
Let it soften if you can.

Your eyes—resting behind closed lids.
Your jaw—slightly open, teeth apart.

Your neck—supported, letting go.

Your shoulders—dropping away from your ears.

Your arms—heavy, resting.
Your hands—relaxed, fingers naturally curled.

Your chest—rising and falling with breath.
Your belly—soft, easy.

Your hips—heavy, supported.

Your thighs—relaxing into the floor.
Your knees—letting go of holding.

Your calves—resting.
Your feet—falling outward.

Your toes—relaxed.

Now sense your whole body at once—
breathing, resting, present.

If your mind wandered during this scan,
just notice that, and return to body awareness.

When you're ready,
slowly bring movement back—
wiggle toes and fingers,
stretch gently,
open your eyes when ready.

Notice how your body feels now.
```

### Grounding Practice (5 minutes)

**Purpose:** Connect to the present through body awareness

```
Sit comfortably with feet flat on the floor.

Notice where your body makes contact—
feet on floor,
body on chair,
hands resting.

Bring attention to your feet.
Feel the contact with the floor.
Notice the support beneath you.

Sense into your feet—
toes, arches, heels.
Feel them connected to the earth.

Move attention up through your legs—
calves, knees, thighs.
Feel their weight and presence.

Notice your hips and pelvis—
heavy, supported by the chair.

Sense your spine—
from base to head,
strong but not rigid.

Notice your shoulders—
dropping, relaxing.
Your arms, hands—resting, supported.

Your head—balanced on your spine,
neither forward nor back.

Feel your whole body sitting here,
supported by the chair,
connected to the earth.

Present, stable, grounded.

Take a few breaths,
feeling this sense of grounding.

Carry this awareness with you
as you move forward.
```

### Progressive Muscle Relaxation (10 minutes)

**Purpose:** Release tension through conscious relaxation

```
Lie down comfortably or sit in a chair.

Start with your hands.
Make a tight fist—
squeeze, squeeze, squeeze.

Notice the tension.
Hold for 5 seconds.

Release.
Feel the tension drain away.
Notice the difference between tension and relaxation.

Move to your shoulders.
Raise them toward your ears—
tight, tense, holding.

Notice the tension.
Hold for 5 seconds.

Drop them suddenly.
Let go completely.
Feel the release.

Your brow—
scrunch it up tight.
Hold.

Release.
Smooth your forehead,
let all tension go.

Your jaw—
clench your teeth tight.
Hold.

Release.
Let your jaw hang slack,
teeth slightly apart.

Continue through your body—
stomach, buttocks, legs, toes—
tensing and releasing
each muscle group.

Finally, sense your whole body—
more relaxed, at ease.

Rest here for a few moments,
enjoying the sensation of release.

When you're ready,
gently move and open your eyes.
```

## Meditation Scripts

### Loving-Kindness Meditation (10 minutes)

**Purpose:** Cultivate warmth and compassion

```
Sit comfortably.
Close your eyes or soften your gaze.

Take a few breaths,
settling into presence.

Begin with yourself.
Silently repeat:

May I be happy.
May I be healthy.
May I be safe.
May I live with ease.

Notice any resistance—
thoughts that you don't deserve this,
feelings of awkwardness.

Let those be there too,
and continue repeating:

May I be happy.
May I be healthy.
May I be safe.
May I live with ease.

Now think of someone you care about.
Picture them in your mind.

Repeat for them:

May you be happy.
May you be healthy.
May you be safe.
May you live with ease.

Now think of someone neutral—
a neighbor, a stranger you passed.

Repeat for them:

May you be happy.
May you be healthy.
May you be safe.
May you live with ease.

Now think of someone difficult—
someone who's challenged you.

Repeat for them if you can:

May you be happy.
May you be healthy.
May you be safe.
May you live with ease.

If this feels hard,
notice that,
and return to yourself or someone easier.

Finally, extend to all beings:

May all beings be happy.
May all beings be healthy.
May all beings be safe.
May all beings live with ease.

Sit with the feelings arising—
warmth, connection,
perhaps resistance.

All are welcome here.

When you're ready,
open your eyes
and carry this kindness forward.
```

### Open Awareness Meditation (15 minutes)

**Purpose:** Practice spacious, undirected presence

```
Sit comfortably.
Spine straight, hands resting.

Close your eyes or soften your gaze.

Take a few breaths,
settling into presence.

Now, instead of focusing on one thing,
open your awareness to everything.

Notice sounds—
near, far, loud, soft.
Let them come and go.

Notice thoughts—
arising, passing, like clouds.
Don't follow them, just notice.

Notice emotions—
present, changing, flowing.
Allow them without holding on.

Notice body sensations—
subtle, obvious, shifting.
Let them be as they are.

Rest in open awareness—
whatever arises, let it be.
whatever passes, let it go.

You are the space
in which all experiences appear and disappear.

Nothing to fix,
nowhere to go,
just this—
here, now, aware.

When you notice you've gotten caught
in thoughts or stories,
just return to open awareness—
sounds, sensations, breath,
all included, none fixated on.

Continue resting in this spacious presence,
observing without grasping,
aware without narrowing.

When you're ready,
slowly return to focus,
open your eyes,
and carry this sense of openness
into your day.
```

## Stress-Reduction Techniques

### STOP Practice (2 minutes)

**Purpose:** Reset during moments of stress

```
S - STOP

Right now, just stop.
Pause whatever you're doing.
Put down your phone, stop typing,
freeze movement.

T - TAKE A BREATH

Take one conscious breath.
Feel the air enter and leave.
Notice your body for a moment.

O - OBSERVE

Notice what's happening:
Thoughts—racing? Worrying?
Emotions—anxious? Frustrated?
Body—tense? Shallow breath?
Just observe, don't fix.

P - PROCEED

Now, what matters to you right now?
What action aligns with your values?
What's one intentional step?

Take that step mindfully,
present to what you're doing.
```

### RAIN Practice (5-10 minutes)

**Purpose:** Work with difficult emotions

```
R - RECOGNIZE

Notice what's difficult right now.
Name it if you can:
"This is anger."
"This is anxiety."
"This is sadness."

A - ALLOW

Let it be here.
Don't try to fix it,
push it away,
or analyze it.
Just allow it space.

I - INVESTIGATE

With kindness and curiosity:
Where do you feel this in your body?
What thoughts accompany it?
What might it need or want?
What's it trying to protect or communicate?

N - NURTURE

What kindness can you offer yourself right now?
What would help?
A hand on your heart?
A compassionate phrase?
A moment of rest?

Offer that to yourself,
being as kind as you would to a friend.

Rest here as long as needed,
then return to your day
when you're ready.
```

### Grounding for Anxiety (3 minutes)

**Purpose:** Return to the present during anxiety

```
Notice you're feeling anxious—
racing thoughts, tight chest, shallow breath.

Pause.
Bring attention to your feet.
Feel them on the floor,
connected, supported.

Look around you.
Name 5 things you can see:
1. ___________________
2. ___________________
3. ___________________
4. ___________________
5. ___________________

Name 4 things you can touch:
1. ___________________
2. ___________________
3. ___________________
4. ___________________

Name 3 things you can hear:
1. ___________________
2. ___________________
3. ___________________

Name 2 things you can smell:
1. ___________________
2. ___________________

Name 1 thing you can taste:
1. ___________________

Notice yourself here, now,
in this room, in this body.
The anxiety may still be present,
but so are you—
grounded, present, aware.
```

## Daily Life Practices

### Mindful Eating (One Meal)

**Purpose:** Bring presence to eating

```
Before you begin,
pause and look at your food.
Notice colors, textures, arrangement.

Notice any smells arising.
Notice hunger level—
ravenous? Moderate? Present?

Slowly take your first bite.
Don't swallow yet.
Notice flavors, textures, temperature.

Chew slowly and consciously.
Notice how the flavors change.

When you swallow,
notice that movement.

Put your utensil down between bites.
Take a breath.
Notice hunger levels changing.

Continue eating this way—
slowly, consciously, present to each bite.

Notice when you feel satisfied,
not necessarily full.
Stop there.

Reflect on the experience:
Did you notice anything new?
How was this different from usual eating?
```

### Mindful Walking (5-10 minutes)

**Purpose:** Practice mindfulness in movement

```
Begin standing still.
Feel your feet on the ground,
your body balanced.

Notice your intention to walk.

Lift your right foot.
Notice the sensation—
toes leaving the ground,
weight shifting.

Move your foot forward.
Notice the movement through air.

Place your foot down.
Heel first, then roll to toes.
Notice contact with ground.

Shift your weight forward.
Now the left foot—
lift, move, place, shift.

Continue walking slowly,
mindful of each step.

Notice your whole body moving—
arms swinging, hips shifting,
breath flowing.

When mind wanders,
return to the sensation of walking.

Continue for the duration,
then pause and stand still.

Notice the effects—
more present? Grounded? Connected?

Carry this mindful presence forward.
```

### Mindful Pause (30 seconds)

**Purpose:** Brief resets throughout the day

```
Set an intention to pause
at specific times—
before meals,
between tasks,
when transitioning.

In each pause:

Stop moving for 30 seconds.
Feel your feet on the floor.
Take three conscious breaths.
Notice what's present right now.

Then continue your day,
renewed in presence.

Use these pauses as anchors—
touchpoints returning you
to here, now, aware.
```

## Post-Practice Reflection Questions

After any mindfulness practice, consider:

1. **What did you notice?**
   - In your body?
   - In your thoughts?
   - In your emotions?

2. **What was challenging?**
   - Wandering mind?
   - Restlessness?
   - Sleepiness?
   - Discomfort?

3. **What came easily?**
   - Focusing?
   - Relaxing?
   - Staying present?

4. **What would help next time?**
   - Shorter practice?
   - Different posture?
   - Eyes open?
   - Different time of day?

5. **What can you carry forward?**
   - Sense of calm?
   - Present-moment awareness?
   - Kindness toward yourself?

## Safety Notes

**If during practice you experience:**

- Overwhelming emotions
- Trauma memories surfacing
- Increased anxiety or panic
- Physical discomfort

**Pause the practice.**
Return to ordinary awareness.
Ground yourself in the room.

**Consider:**
- Shorter practices next time
- Eyes-open practices
- Focusing on external sounds
- Working with a professional

These scripts are educational tools.
For ongoing support with trauma,
anxiety, or depression,
consider a licensed mental health professional.
